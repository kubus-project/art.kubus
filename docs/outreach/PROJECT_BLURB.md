## One line

art.kubus is an open-source Flutter app for discovering, documenting and connecting public art on a MapLibre map.

## Short (about 50 words)

art.kubus is an open-source public art map. A single Flutter codebase runs on Android, iOS, web and desktop, and uses MapLibre with custom vector styles over OpenStreetMap-based tiles. People find artworks nearby, see their sources and credits, and follow artists and institutions. AR and decentralized infrastructure are optional layers.
